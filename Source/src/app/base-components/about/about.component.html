About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California, Riverside, currently pursuing my undergraduate degree with a strong focus in precision manufacturing and mechatronics. My academic foundation in mechanical engineering has shaped my ability to approach engineering challenges with a perspective informed by real-world experience and continuous learning through iteration and problem-solving.

I'm driven by a passion for truly understanding mechanical systems, how they work, why they work, and how every component interacts. I love taking that knowledge and weaving it into my own projects to create something original and functional.

My experience spans machine design, mechatronics, and hands-on application, which has given me the ability to quickly grasp the inner workings of mechanisms. This has allowed me to diagnose issues efficiently and creatively repurpose elements of existing systems.

I'm always seeking opportunities to apply and expand these skills in meaningful, forward-thinking projects—whether in aerial systems, precision manufacturing, or innovative mechanical designs that can have a tangible real-world impact.
