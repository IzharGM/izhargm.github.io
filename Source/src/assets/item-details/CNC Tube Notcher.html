CNC Tube Notcher

Compact Precision Plasma Tube Notching System

Introduction

A CNC tube notcher is a precision fabrication tool designed to plasma cut accurate notches into circular tubing, allowing the tubes to fit together seamlessly and be welded to form strong, reliable joints.

Tube notching involves removing material from a tube at specific angles to create profiles that match the geometry of the member it will join, eliminating gaps and enabling high-quality welds in structures like frames and roll cages, where strength and rigidity are critical.

A CNC tube notcher automates this process by controlling the vertical position of the plasma gun, the rotation of the tube, and the linear movement of the tube using G-code, similar to the operation of a 3D printer, producing consistent, repeatable notches across a range of sizes and angles while drastically improving efficiency compared to manual methods.

Unlike many commercially available CNC tube notchers, this design prioritizes a compact form factor, making it accessible for garages or workshops that cannot accommodate machines the size of a car.
