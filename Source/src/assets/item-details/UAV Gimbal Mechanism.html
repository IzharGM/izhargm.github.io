UAV Gimbal Mechanism

2026 Student Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (SUAS) Competition

Introduction

For the 2026 Student Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Systems Competition, the gimbal plays a critical role in enabling the UAV to search, detect, and accurately interact with mission targets within the designated search boundary.

The gimbal is responsible for providing stabilized visual data of the surrounding terrain, allowing the aircraft to identify targets while maintaining stable flight at altitude. This visual information must be seamlessly integrated with the flight controller, which processes the data to make precise adjustments to the UAV's position, orientation, and navigation path.

Once a target is confirmed, coordination between the gimbal, flight controller, and payload mechanism is essential to ensure the aircraft is correctly positioned for payload deployment. The payload mechanism relies on this shared system awareness to deliver two distinct payloads to their intended locations accurately and safely.

Successful mission execution depends on the reliable interaction of these systems, where visual sensing, autonomous flight control, and payload delivery work together as a unified system. I will be taking inspiration from the Trillium HD25-XV when building this gimbal given its aerodynamic and compact form factor.
