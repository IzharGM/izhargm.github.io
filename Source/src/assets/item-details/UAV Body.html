2026 Student Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (SUAS) Competition

Introduction

For the 2026 Student Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (SUAS) Competition, a primary mission of the UAV is the accurate delivery of payloads to designated targets from an altitude of 150 feet.

To ensure proper and efficient execution of this task, the drone must carry several essential components, including a flight controller that processes sensor data to make fine adjustments for stable flight, a 3-axis gimbal that scans the surrounding terrain and provides visual feedback for navigation, a payload mechanism capable of holding and deploying two objects to their designated locations, and other critical electrical systems.

A rigid, lightweight frame that can efficiently package and protect these components from environmental exposure, withstand impact in the event of a crash, and handle the aerodynamic and structural loads experienced in flight is vital to the successful completion of the competition objectives.
